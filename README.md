# 20 November 2026 — World News in One Image

A news-magazine front page summing up Friday, 20 November 2026, in a single
picture. The masthead names the day, and five labelled panels cover the day's
most consequential stories across regions and fields:

1. **Shenzhen Summit** — Presidents Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first-ever
   three-way meeting in Shenzhen and sign a joint peace declaration, with no
   breakthrough on Ukraine, Iran or nuclear arms.
2. **ICJ: Myanmar Guilty of Genocide** — The International Court of Justice
   rules, 9–6, that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — the
   court's first such finding against a state.
3. **Missile Hits Riyadh Tower** — A Houthi missile damages the Public
   Investment Fund tower in Riyadh's financial district.
4. **Anthropic IPO Soars, Markets Slide** — Anthropic closes ~17% above its
   IPO price on its Nasdaq debut while stocks fall for a fifth straight day
   (S&P 500 −2%, Nikkei −4.8%).
5. **GTA 6 Launches — $2.3 Billion** — Grand Theft Auto VI goes on sale
   worldwide after 13 years, topping $2.3 billion in sales.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, fully deterministic — every run writes the
exact same bytes. The image is a PNG, 1024×682 pixels, under 1 MiB.
